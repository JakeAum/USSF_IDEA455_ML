# Applied ML / AI for Organic Guardian Analytics
Design 4 Defense (CSU IDEA 455) Final Deliverable

# Vision
The United States Space Force (USSF) aims to invest in the teaching, training, and promotion of organic Guardians. This investment is backed by measurable proof that such efforts will yield direct returns within the service. By focusing on the development and growth of Guardians, the USSF seeks to enhance its capabilities and ensure a robust and effective force.

# Mission
Our mission is to develop a comprehensive methodology and plan to deploy a system that collects both quantitative and qualitative data for Guardians entering and exiting the USSF service. This system will provide valuable insights into the training and education processes, helping to refine and improve them over time.

# Significance
This project presents a unique opportunity as the USSF was established only five years ago, in [MONTH, YEAR]. With the average contract length of an Enlisted Guardian being six years, this cohort represents a starting point (n=0) for gathering data and closing the feedback loop on training and education processes. Unlike more established branches like the Air Force, the USSF lacks a heritage of training and education, making this initiative crucial for its development.
As the first cohort of organic Guardians approaches their decision window to either renew their contracts with the USSF or exit the service, this project aims to provide the necessary data and insights to support their decision-making process and improve the overall effectiveness of the USSF.
